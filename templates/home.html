Mildew Detection System

This project is designed to help farmers identify powdery mildew on cherry leaves using a Convolutional Neural Network (CNN). The system allows users to upload a 256x256 cherry leaf image and receive a prediction of whether the leaf is "Healthy" or has "Powdery Mildew" with 99.37% accuracy.

Client's Requirements

The client, a group of cherry farmers, requires a system to:

  • Accurately identify powdery mildew on cherry leaves to enable timely treatment and protect crop yield.
  • Provide a user-friendly interface for uploading images and viewing predictions.
  • Offer insights into the model’s performance to ensure trust in the predictions.
  • Include detailed information about the dataset used for training and testing.
