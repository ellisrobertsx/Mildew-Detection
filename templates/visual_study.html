Visual Study: Differentiating Healthy and Mildew-Affected Cherry Leaves

This page lists findings from a study on visually differentiating healthy cherry leaves from those affected by powdery mildew, which informed the development of the MildewCNN model.

Visual Characteristics

  • Healthy Cherry Leaves: Typically exhibit a vibrant green color, smooth texture, and uniform surface without spots or discoloration.
  • Powdery Mildew-Affected Cherry Leaves: Show white or grayish powdery spots on the surface, often on the upper side, with potential yellowing or browning around affected areas.

Model Insights

The MildewCNN model was trained to detect these visual differences by focusing on:

  • Color Patterns: The model identifies the green color of healthy leaves versus the white/gray spots of mildew.
  • Texture Features: The convolutional layers detect the smooth texture of healthy leaves versus the powdery texture of mildew-affected leaves.
  • Spatial Distribution: The model learns the distribution of spots, which are typically scattered on mildew-affected leaves but absent on healthy ones.

These findings were validated by examining the model’s predictions on the test set, achieving 99.37% accuracy, confirming its ability to differentiate based on these visual characteristics.
